# Spend is a charge ledger

Spend in recurring is the sum of confirmed Singapore-dollar **charges**, each tied to a renewal date. The **billed price** (amount and currency on the invoice) is stored as a label and never enters totals or charts. When you change a subscription’s current SGD amount, only later charges use the new figure; past charges stay unless you edit that charge directly. We rejected live currency conversion and a single editable amount that rewrites history, because card charges in SGD rarely match mid-market rates and retroactive edits would make cash charts untrustworthy.
